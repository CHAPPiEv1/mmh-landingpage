Our Departments

Comprehensive Care Services

Providing accessible healthcare solutions tailored to every family in Malasiqui and neighboring municipalities.

24/7 Emergency Care

Round-the-clock emergency medical support equipped with trauma response, minor surgical care, and emergency triage staff.

Outpatient Department (OPD)

Scheduled doctor consultations, health screenings, follow-up examinations, and preventive medical advice.

View Schedule

Maternity & Child Care

Comprehensive prenatal checkups, normal spontaneous delivery services, newborn screening, and pediatric care.

Laboratory & Imaging

Essential clinical laboratory testing (blood, urinalysis, stool analysis) and X-ray diagnostic imaging services.

Hospital Pharmacy

In-house dispensing of essential medications, emergency drugs, and prescribed treatments for inpatients and OPD.

PhilHealth & Malasakit Desk

On-site assistance for PhilHealth membership coverage, financial aid processing, and medical social work.

Patient Information

Patient & Visitor Guidelines

Essential information to ensure a smooth, organized, and hassle-free visit to Malasiqui Municipal Hospital.

Checklist for OPD & Admission

  • Valid Government ID:

    PhilSys ID, Driver's License, Voter's ID, or Senior Citizen ID.

  • PhilHealth Member Record:

    MDR form or PhilHealth ID card for benefit claiming.

  • Previous Medical Records:

    Laboratory test results, X-ray films, or prescription slips.

  • Hospital Yellow Card / Record:

    If you are a returning patient at MMH.

Afternoon Visiting Schedule

Visitor Safety Guidelines:

  • Maximum of 2 visitors per patient allowed inside the ward at a time.
  • Wearing of face masks is recommended inside clinical wards.
  • Children under 12 years old are discouraged from visiting ward areas for safety.

PhilHealth & Medical Social Assistance

Malasiqui Municipal Hospital provides full assistance for PhilHealth billing coverage and local social services support for indigent residents.

PhilHealth Express Desk

Assists with membership verification, benefit entitlement claims, and No-Balance-Billing (NBB) eligibility.

Medical Social Worker Office

Evaluates eligibility for municipal medical assistance grants and financial relief programs.
